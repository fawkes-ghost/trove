Trove is a prize draw, and we follow the DCMS voluntary code for prize draws. These are the controls you can set and the support that is there if you need it.

## The credit card limit

1. The most you can spend on entries by credit card is {t.creditCardMonthlyCap} in any calendar month, across every Trove draw.
2. There is no way to raise this limit.

## Set your own limit

1. You can set a monthly spending limit lower than the credit card limit, and a limit on the number of entries you hold in any draw.
2. A lower limit takes effect at once. A higher limit takes effect after a cooling-off period of 24 hours. TODO: confirm the period with the solicitor.
3. The most any one person can hold in a single draw is {t.maxPerPerson} entries, paid and postal together, whatever limit you set.

## Take a break or close your account

1. You can suspend your account for a period you choose. While it is suspended you cannot enter any draw, and we do not send you marketing.
2. You can close your account at any time. We confirm the closure by email, and we do not reopen it at your request for six months. TODO: confirm the period with the solicitor.
3. To suspend or close your account, write to {t.contactEmail} and we act within one working day.

## Support

1. If you are worried about how you or someone else enters draws or gambles, GamCare offers free, confidential support at [{t.gamCare}]({t.gamCare}), and GambleAware at [{t.gambleAware}]({t.gambleAware}).
2. You must be {t.minimumAge} or over to enter. We verify age before a prize is awarded.
