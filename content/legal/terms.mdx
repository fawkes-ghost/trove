## Definitions

- **Cap.** The most entries a draw can hold, paid and postal together. For {t.escapeName} the cap is {t.cap}.
- **Cash alternative.** The fixed cash payment described in clause 6, available to the winner instead of the escape.
- **Draw.** The random selection of one winning entry from every entry in the ledger.
- **Entry.** One valid submission into the draw, made online by payment or by post for free. Every entry has identical odds.
- **Escape.** The prize described in clause 5.
- **Founding friend.** A person who joined the waitlist before entries opened.
- **Ledger.** The published record of entries taken against the cap.
- **Longstop.** The date, set once when entries open and never moved later, on which entries close if the cap has not been reached.
- **Promoter, we, us, our.** {t.promoter}.
- **You, your.** The person entering the draw.

## How to enter

### Paid entry

1. Paid entries are made on the website while entries are open.
2. A single entry costs {t.entryPrice}. Entries are also sold in bundles: {t.bundles.join('; ')}.
3. Each entry is given a unique number in the ledger and has identical odds to every other entry.
4. The most any one person may hold in a draw is {t.maxPerPerson} entries, paid and postal together. At the single entry price that is {t.spendCeiling}. Entries beyond the limit are refused and refunded.
5. Founding friends are told first when entries open and may enter before the public opening. They receive no additional entries and no change to their odds.

### Free entry by post

1. No purchase is necessary. Anyone eligible may enter for free by post.
2. Send a postcard with your full name, email address, date of birth and the name of the escape to {t.postalAddress}.
3. Each postcard is one entry. One postcard per envelope.
4. Every postal entry is given a unique number in the ledger and enters the draw inside the same cap with identical odds to a paid entry.
5. The postal route closes when the cap is reached or on the longstop date, whichever comes first, and never earlier. Postcards that arrive after the close are not entered.

### Identical odds

1. Every valid entry, paid or postal, has exactly the same chance of being drawn. The method of entry has no effect on odds. The worst-case odds for one entry, if every entry in the cap is taken, are {t.worstCaseOdds}.

3. Our decision on matters of fact concerning the draw is final, subject to your statutory rights.
