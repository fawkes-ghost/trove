- **Cash alternative.** The fixed cash payment in clause 6, which the winner can take instead of the escape.
- **Closing date.** The date, published once when entries open and never moved later, on which entries close if they have not all gone.
- **Draw.** Picking one winning entry at random from all the entries, paid and postal.
- **Entry.** One valid entry into the draw, bought online or sent free by post. Every entry has the same chance of winning.
- **Escape.** The prize described in clause 5.
- **Founding friend.** Someone who joined the waitlist before entries opened.
- **Maximum entries.** The most entries a draw can ever have, paid and postal together. For {t.escapeName} it is {t.cap}.

## How to enter

### Paid entry

1. You can buy entries on the website while entries are open.
2. One entry costs {t.entryPrice}. Entries are also sold in bundles: {t.bundles.join('; ')}.
3. Each entry gets its own number and has the same chance of winning as every other entry. While entries are open we publish how many have been taken.
4. One person can have up to {t.maxPerPerson} entries in a draw, paid and postal together. At the single entry price that is {t.spendCeiling}. Entries over the limit are refused and refunded.
5. Founding friends hear first when entries open and may enter before everyone else. They get no extra entries and no better chance of winning.

### Free entry by post

1. You don’t have to buy anything to enter. Anyone who can enter may enter free by post.
2. Send a postcard with your full name, email address, date of birth and the name of the escape to {t.postalAddress}.
3. Each postcard is one entry. One postcard per envelope.
4. Each postal entry gets its own number, counts towards the same maximum entries, and has the same chance of winning as a paid entry.
5. Postal entries close at the same moment as paid entries: when all the entries have gone or on the closing date, whichever comes first, and never earlier. Postcards that arrive after that are not entered.

### Same chance for every entry

1. Every valid entry, paid or postal, has exactly the same chance of being drawn. How you enter makes no difference. With one entry, your chance is at least {t.worstCaseOdds}, which is your chance if all {t.cap} entries are taken.

## When the draw closes

1. Entries close when all {t.cap} entries have gone, or on the closing date, whichever comes first.
2. We publish the closing date when entries open and never move it later. We never extend a closing date.
3. There is no minimum number of entries. There is always one winner, however many entries are taken. The prize never rolls over, and we never cancel a draw because too few entries were taken.

## The draw

1. We draw one entry at random from all the entries, paid and postal, using a method that can be independently checked.
2. We publish the winning entry number and how it was drawn on the result page.
3. Our decision on the facts of the draw is final. This does not affect your statutory rights.

4. We publish the winner’s first name and county on the result page. Email <a href={`mailto:${t.contactEmail}`}>{t.contactEmail}</a> if you have a strong reason for us not to.
