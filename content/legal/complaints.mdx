If something has gone wrong, tell us. We would rather hear about it and put it right.

## How to complain

1. Email <a href={`mailto:${t.contactEmail}`}>{t.contactEmail}</a> with “Complaint” in the subject line, or write to us at the address on the contact page.
2. Tell us your name, the email address on your account if you have one, what happened and what you would like us to do.

## What happens next

1. We acknowledge your complaint within two working days.
2. We send you a full answer within fourteen days. If we need longer, we tell you why and when you will hear from us.
3. A named person who was not involved in what went wrong looks at your complaint.

## About a draw

1. If your complaint is about how a draw was run, entered or decided, we look at the draw records, including the entry list and the record of the draw itself, and tell you what they show.
2. The terms explain how winners are chosen and contacted. Read the <a href={t.significantConditionsPath}>key terms</a> and the <a href="/legal/terms">full terms</a>.

## If you are still unhappy

1. About our advertising: you can complain to the Advertising Standards Authority at <a href="https://www.asa.org.uk/make-a-complaint.html">asa.org.uk</a>.
2. About your personal data: you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at <a href="https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/">ico.org.uk</a>.
3. About anything else, as a consumer: the Citizens Advice consumer helpline on 0808 223 1133 can advise you and refer the matter to Trading Standards.
