The terms that matter most, in one place. The [full terms](/legal/terms) apply in full.

## Who can enter

Anyone aged {t.minimumAge} or over who lives in the {t.residency}. People who work for Trove, and the people they live with, can’t enter.

## How to enter

Buy entries on this website while entries are open, or enter free by post. Each postcard is one entry, with the same chance of winning as a paid entry, and counts towards the same total. See [free entry by post](/free-entry-by-post).

## How many entries

There are never more than {t.cap} entries in {t.escapeName}, paid and postal together, and that number is never raised. One person can have up to {t.maxPerPerson} entries.

## Your chances

Every entry has the same chance. With one entry, your chance is at least {t.worstCaseOdds}. That’s if all {t.cap} entries are taken. If fewer are, your chance is better.

## When entries close

When all {t.cap} entries have gone, or on the closing date we publish, whichever comes first. We never extend the closing date. There is always a winner and the prize never rolls over.

## The prize

{t.prizeDescription.join('. ')}. A {t.prizeValue} prize, of which {t.prizeCash} is cash. The stay must be taken {t.stayWindow}. We send the winner at least {t.datesOffered} sets of dates. They have {t.claimWindowDays} days from then to choose one, or to take {t.cashAlternative} in cash instead of the whole prize. If they choose neither, they get the cash.

## The winner

We draw one entry at random from all the entries, paid and postal. We email the winner, who must reply within {t.winnerResponseDays} days. We publish the winning entry number and how it was drawn on the result page.

## Where the money goes

{t.givingLine} We’ll name the charities once our agreement with them is signed, and we publish what was given after every draw.

## Who runs the draw

{t.promoter}. Email {t.contactEmail} with any question.
