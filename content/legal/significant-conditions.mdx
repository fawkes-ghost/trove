The conditions that matter most, in one place. The [full terms](/legal/terms) govern.

## Who can enter

Residents of the {t.residency} aged {t.minimumAge} or over. People who work for Trove, and their households, cannot enter.

## How to enter

Buy entries on the website while entries are open, or enter for free by post. Each postcard is one entry with identical odds inside the same cap. See [free entry by post](/free-entry-by-post).

## How many entries

The cap for {t.escapeName} is {t.cap} entries, paid and postal together, and is never raised. The most any one person may hold is {t.maxPerPerson} entries.

## The odds

Every entry has identical odds. The worst-case odds for one entry, if every entry in the cap is taken, are {t.worstCaseOdds}.

## When the draw closes

When the cap is reached or on the published longstop date, whichever comes first. We never extend a closing date. One winner is guaranteed; there is no rollover.

## The prize

{t.prizeDescription.join('. ')}. A {t.prizeValue} prize, of which {t.prizeCash} is cash. The winner may choose a cash alternative of {t.cashAlternative} instead of the escape, within {t.claimWindowDays} days of the draw.

## The winner

One entry is drawn at random from every entry in the ledger. The winner is notified by email and must respond within {t.winnerResponseDays} days. The winning entry number and how it was drawn are published on the result page.

## Where the money goes

{t.charityPence} in every pound of entry sales goes to community and countryside causes at the destination. The partner is named once the agreement is signed, and we publish what was given after every draw.

## The promoter

{t.promoter}. Write to {t.contactEmail} with any question.
