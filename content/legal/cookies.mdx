Trove sets as little in your browser as it can. Nothing used for analytics is stored until you accept it, and you can change your mind at any time.

## What is always stored

These are strictly necessary, so they do not need your consent.

1. **Your cookie choice.** We store whether you accepted or declined analytics in your browser’s local storage, under the name trove:consent, so we do not ask again on every page. It stays until you clear your browser’s storage.
2. **How you arrived.** We hold the campaign tags, referring page and landing page of your visit in your browser’s session storage, under the name trove:attribution. It is deleted when you close the tab, and it leaves your browser only if you join the founding friends list.
3. **The form check.** Cloudflare Turnstile runs when you send a form, to check you are a person. It may store a short-lived token in your browser to do this.

## What is stored only if you accept

1. **Google Analytics.** If you accept analytics, Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_ followed by an identifier. They tell visits apart so we can count them, and they last up to two years. If you decline, the analytics tag does not load and these cookies are never set.

## Changing your choice

1. To change your choice, clear this site’s data in your browser settings and the banner will ask you again on your next visit.
2. Questions about this page go to <a href={`mailto:${t.contactEmail}`}>{t.contactEmail}</a>.
